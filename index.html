FTC BIOBUZZ · 2026–27

Practise the match before the match.

DriveForge is a driving simulator for FTC teams: the real BIOBUZZ field, the real rules, and the robot your team actually built, in your browser.

01 · Your robot

Your CAD, not ours.

Upload your team's STEP or GLB export from Onshape, SolidWorks, Fusion or Inventor. DriveForge reads the drivetrain, intake and launcher from the parts, proposes a setup, and lets you correct it, so you practise with the robot you will bring to the match.

02 · Physics

Physics a driver can trust.

Rigid-body physics at 60 steps a second on the detailed field. Wheels, rollers and the launcher are modelled from the real parts: a POLLEN leaves the flywheel at the speed the wheel is actually turning, and HIVEs tip when they are knocked. When the sim changes how the game plays, it is measured and written down, never quietly absorbed.

03 · The match

Match night, any night.

  • Match vs CPU against three automated robots, scored by the same rules as a real match.
  • Online rooms to drive with your alliance partner from different computers.
  • Fouls called from the Competition Manual, with the rule number and why.
  • Replays and stats: cycles, shots, makes and idle time, shareable with your team.
  • Autonomous: paste your Pedro Pathing code and watch it run on the field.

04 · Controls

Drive it the way you drive it.

Two gamepads set up like your TeleOp, with field-centric drive and an IMU reset. Or the keyboard, or touch controls on a phone or tablet. It runs on a school laptop, and says so plainly when a machine is too slow.

05 · Privacy

Your CAD stays yours.

A team's CAD never leaves its account. Teammates in a room get collision shapes, never your source files. Anything that would send data outward is opt-in, with the cost stated plainly.
